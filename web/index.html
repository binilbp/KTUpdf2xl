About Us

We are a dedicated team passionate about making academic data accessible and understandable. Our Analyzer tool was born from the need to simplify result analysis for KTU students and faculty, transforming complex grade sheets into clear, actionable insights.

Our Team

Our team consists of skilled developers, UI/UX designers, and data analysts who have first-hand experience with the challenges of the KTU academic system. We are committed to continuous improvement and user-focused development.

Features

Instantly calculate your SGPA and CGPA. Visualize your academic performance over semesters with interactive charts. Compare subject-wise performance and identify trends. Securely store and manage all your result documents in one place.
